# SEO competitor analysis

Who already answers the searches in [`SEO_KEYWORD_MAP.md`](SEO_KEYWORD_MAP.md),
what they offer, and where DoOrDie can honestly compete.

Researched 17 November 2026 by searching the main keywords and reading the
competitors' own home pages. There are no traffic or ranking numbers here: the
project has no paid SEO tool, and anything quoted below comes from the
competitors' public pages at that date. Products change quickly, so re-check
before relying on a detail.

## Who shows up

The results for UPSC tracking searches fall into three groups.

1. **Dedicated tracker websites.** Small, mostly free products, many started in
   the last two years. They are the direct competitors for the product keywords
   (cluster A, and the tool rows of B to E).
2. **Store apps.** Android and iOS apps whose store listings rank for "app"
   searches.
3. **Content publishers.** Coaching institutes and edtech companies, plus Quora
   and document-sharing sites. They own the how-to searches (cluster F and the
   guides) and the exam-date searches.

Store listings rank for "app" searches because Google shows app results for
them. DoOrDie is a web app that can be installed from the browser, so it

Trusted Web Activity wrapping the existing app) is possible later and is in the
roadmap's "bigger bets".

## 3. Content publishers

For the how-to searches the results are led by coaching institutes and edtech
companies: Vajiram & Ravi, Vajirao Institute, Physics Wallah (pw.live and
pwonlyias.com), Testbook, Unacademy, Sleepy Classes, Oswaal360, Plutus IAS and
Adda247. Quora threads appear for nearly every question, and Scribd uploads
appear for "pdf" and "sheet" searches. A few independent writers also rank,
such as upscprep.com for mock analysis and csewhy.com for study plans.

What their pages have in common:

- The exam year in the title ("UPSC Study Plan 2027"), updated every cycle.
- Long pages that answer many related questions at once.
- Strong domains with years of links.
- Specific claims, for example a recommended daily-hours range or a target
  accuracy percentage, often without a source.

DoOrDie cannot match their domain strength soon. Its guides compete on method
instead: each one is a practical system the reader can apply that day, with
sources for factual claims and a free tool or feature that does the tedious part.

## Where DoOrDie stands

**Strengths that can be stated honestly**

- One private account covers the daily journal, syllabus, revision, mistakes,
  mocks, goals, reviews, habits, wellbeing and reflections. Most of the products
  above focus on a few of these, usually syllabus, hours and revision.
- Free, with no ads, no paid tier and no referral gate.
- Runs in any modern browser, installs as an app, works offline and syncs
  across devices.
- Analytics never runs inside the app.
- The public pages say what the product does not do.

**Gaps (where competitors are ahead)**

- No previous-year question bank or practice tests. DoOrDie records mocks taken
  elsewhere.
- No study timer. Several competitors make the timer the centre of tracking.
- No mentors, groups or community features.
- No Hindi interface.
- No store listing.
- A new domain with no links. Every competitor above has a head start.

The timer is the gap most often mentioned in competitors' pitches, and it fits
the product. It is a product decision for the owner, not an SEO task, so it is
listed under "bigger bets" in the roadmap and was not built here.

## What this means for the SEO plan

1. **Don't copy the page-farm approach.** Thousands of thin question pages need
   content DoOrDie does not have, and search engines treat unhelpful pages at
   scale as spam.
2. **Compete where specific beats big:** exact-match product pages ("syllabus
   tracker", "revision tracker"), free tools (countdown, revision calculator,
   templates) and method guides that the big publishers cover only in passing.
3. **Make every guide lead to one feature,** so readers who arrive with a
   question can see a tool that answers it.
4. **Keep dates current.** The countdown and calendar pages must be updated the
   day UPSC publishes the CSE 2027 notification (13 January 2027), and every
   "2027" mention needs a review after Prelims.
5. **Earn links honestly:** being listed in "free UPSC tools" roundups, the
   free tools being useful enough to share, and helpful answers in aspirant
   communities that link only when the link answers the question.
